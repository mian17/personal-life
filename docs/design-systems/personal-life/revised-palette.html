Palette

Ratios are measured against the card surface of the same theme. Text pairs clear 4.5:1; control boundaries clear 3:1. The hairline is decorative and carries no meaning on its own, so it is held to no ratio.

The screen ground is Page: warm off-white in light, soft charcoal in dark, painted edge to edge at 414px. Groups, cards and swatches sit above it on Surface, so the ground shows through the gaps between them.
